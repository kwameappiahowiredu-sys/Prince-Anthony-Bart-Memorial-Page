Biography

Anthony Bart-Appiah, affectionately known as “Prince Anthony,” was born on 8th November 1981 to Kweku Bart-Appiah and Adjoa Amponsah. He was a devoted husband to Hon. Kayra Harding, former Congresswoman and Deputy Speaker of the National Assembly of Panama, and a loving father of two sons, Caleb Bart-Appiah and Ronn Bart-Appiah.

He was a nephew of the Akwamuhene, Odeneho Kwafo Akoto III, and a grandson of the Akwamuhemea, Okokodurufo Ohemea Afrakoma II.

Professionally, Prince Anthony served with the British Army, where he distinguished himself through discipline, dedication, and professionalism. Beyond his military service, he developed a deep passion for tourism, cultural heritage, and promoting meaningful connections between Ghana and the global African diaspora.

His unwavering love for Ghana and his strong association with people of African descent earned him appointment as a Board Member of the Ghana Tourism Authority, where he contributed to advancing Ghana’s tourism industry and strengthening the country’s engagement with the African diaspora.

As the founder of BridgeZone, Prince Anthony promoted Africa through heritage tourism, diaspora engagement, cultural diplomacy, and investment, helping strengthen connections between Africa and its global diaspora.

Prince Anthony was admired for his humility, generosity, and warm personality. He built lasting friendships and inspired many through his kindness, loyalty, and commitment to family, community, and national service. His life was marked by purpose, compassion, and quiet strength.

Though his earthly journey has come to an end, his legacy lives on in the hearts of his beloved family, friends, colleagues, and all those whose lives he touched. He will be remembered with deep affection, and his life’s work will continue to inspire generations to come.

May his soul rest in perfect peace.
